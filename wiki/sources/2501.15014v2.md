## Problem

Survey of strategies for accelerating deep learning inference under resource constraints, motivated by military tactical edge computing (limited processing, memory, communication). Three-pillar survey: model compression + NAS + deployment/compiler frameworks.

## Taxonomy

**Pillar 1 — Model Compression**:
- Quantization: PTQ (SmoothQuant, AWQ, GPTQ, CVXQ, RPTQ), QAT (LLM-QAT, EfficientQAT), mixed-precision, QLoRA
- Pruning: unstructured, structured, N:M semi-structured
- Knowledge Distillation: logit, feature, federated, CoT distillation for LLMs
- Tensor Decomposition: SVD, Tucker, CP

**Pillar 2 — NAS and HPO**:
- Evolutionary NAS, differentiable NAS (DARTS), hierarchical NAS
- Hardware-aware NAS for specific edge targets
- Neurosymbolic approaches

**Pillar 3 — Compiler and Deployment Frameworks** *(not covered in other wiki surveys)*:
- **TVM** (Apache): hardware-agnostic compilation pipeline; auto-tuning for target hardware
- **TensorRT** (NVIDIA): GPU-optimized inference engine; fuses layers, precision calibration
- **OpenVINO** (Intel): CPU/VPU-optimized inference; supports INT8 calibration

**Integrated pipelines**: combine NAS + compression + compilation for multi-objective deployment.

## Open Challenges Flagged

- Pre-training pruning for massive networks (LLM pruning before training vs. post-training)
- Platform-independent frameworks (current tools are hardware-vendor specific)
- Hierarchical NAS for emerging edge-cloud hybrid architectures

## Significance

The most complete edge AI survey as of 2025 for deployment-focused practitioners. Key addition vs other wiki surveys: deployment compilers (TVM, TensorRT, OpenVINO) — the final translation layer between compressed models and actual hardware speedups. Confirms that compression alone is insufficient: without hardware-tailored compilation, INT8 weights don't automatically yield 4× speedup.

For HaLViT: after applying weight sharing + quantization + pruning, the HaLViT model still needs to be compiled for the target hardware (Raspberry Pi, Jetson Nano, or mobile SoC). TVM's auto-tuning and TensorRT's INT8 calibration are the deployment steps not yet represented in the wiki.
